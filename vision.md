# Vision — ai-gap-radar

## What this is

A public, biweekly builder's radar for the AI agent stack. Each issue answers: what blew up, why, and what gap it leaves open — with evidence, not hype.

## What this is not

- Not another awesome-list (no link dumps without verdicts).
- Not a place for implementation code (that lives in spun-off repos; here only `opportunities/*.md` + a link back).
- Not a daily news feed (biweekly cadence, or it dies).

## What success looks like

1. Every radar issue surfaces ≥1 buildable gap with competitors and an MVP scope.
2. At least one spun-off repo earns its first 500 stars from this repo's audience.
3. Strangers open PRs with corrections and new briefs — the radar runs without the maintainer pushing it.
