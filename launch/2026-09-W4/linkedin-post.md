# LinkedIn post — W4 skills governance (post day after X/dev.to)

Your team installs agent skills the way it installed npm packages in 2015: quickly, and without a policy. The numbers caught up this month.

- NVIDIA's SkillSpector scanner reports ~26% of sampled skills contain vulnerabilities. Every SKILL.md is unsigned code running next to your credentials.
- Cloudflare's security-audit skill rose from ~0.5k stars in June to ~21.4k in September. Vendors now distribute audit playbooks as skills, not SaaS.
- The two largest community collections hold ~269k and ~291k stars. Skills won distribution. Vetting lost.

For managers, the question is concrete: which skills are approved, who approved them, and what permissions do they hold? I could not find a neutral answer, so I started building the evidence base in public: https://github.com/williamlabdev/ai-gap-radar

First output is a proposal for a skill trust gate (lint, sandbox run, permission manifest, score, CI check): https://github.com/williamlabdev/ai-gap-radar/blob/dev/opportunities/skill-security-linter.md
